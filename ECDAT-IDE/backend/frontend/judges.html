For Phase 1, do NOT sound like a technical viva. Judges should first feel: “Real national-security problem → existing tools don't solve end-to-end → these students get the gap → solution is credible + actionable.” Strongest story is not “we built an AI tool” but: “Before quantum-safe, first know where crypto is, what is at risk, what to replace. Today fragmented. QIROVA closes that loop.”

◉ OPENING HOOK — say

“Good morning respected judges. Let me start with a simple situation. Imagine today someone records an encrypted conversation between two government systems. They cannot decrypt it today. But they don't need to. They simply store it. And years later, when sufficiently powerful quantum computers become available, they attempt to decrypt that data. This is called Harvest Now, Decrypt Later. And this changes the quantum threat completely. Because the question is no longer ‘When will quantum computers break encryption?’ The question is: ‘Which of our data is already vulnerable if we wait?’” Pause. “And that is exactly the problem we are solving with QIROVA.”

HNDL + 2029 PQC pressure: Google announced 2029 post-quantum migration target, Mar 2026.

▣ WHAT IS THE PROBLEM? — say

“Today organizations use crypto everywhere. Not one app. Inside source code. Inside compiled software where source may not even be available. Inside containers. Inside TLS certs and connections. Buried in third-party deps.” Point to architecture. “Imagine 10,000 apps. Security wants 3 answers: First — Where exactly are we using crypto? Second — Which are actually at quantum risk? Third — What exactly should we change? Today answered by disconnected tools — one for code, one for binaries, one for TLS, one for vulns — then humans manually join.” Pause. “So problem isn't just RSA/ECC may break. Real problem is no complete map of where crypto is, how important each asset is, when it becomes risky, how to migrate.”

▣ REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE — say

“This isn't theoretical. In Mar 2026 Google announced 2029 PQC migration — quantum progress + error correction + attack-cost estimates changed urgency; already integrating PQC. Cloudflare: by Mar 2025 more than one-third of human web traffic already HNDL-protected via hybrid ML-KEM. Meta describes inventory, risk assessment, deployment, guardrails for enterprise PQC. So world is moving. But: You cannot migrate what you cannot discover. And you cannot prioritize what you cannot measure.” Pause. “That is where our work begins.”

⬢ BACKGROUND STUDY — say

“Before QIROVA we studied 3 domains. QUANTUM → AI/ML → CYBERSECURITY. From quantum: how RSA/ECC hit by Shor, symmetric by Grover, attack-resource estimates, exposure time. From AI/ML: identify crypto by structure/context, not searching ‘RSA’. From cybersecurity: vuln intel, supply-chain, compliance, enterprise practice. Key line: Quantum tells WHAT is dangerous. AI tells HOW to detect accurately. Cybersecurity tells WHY it matters.”

⬢ FIVE GAPS — say

GAP 1 Discovery: “No unified discovery across code, binaries, containers, TLS, deps.” GAP 2 False positives: “Finding word ‘RSA’ ≠ using RSA. Detection without context = noise.” GAP 3 Quantum risk: “Scanners say vulnerable/not. But test app ≠ 20-year classified data.” GAP 4 Remediation: “Tools stop at ‘vulnerable.’ Team still asks ‘What do I change?’” GAP 5 Enterprise: “Gov/CII needs on-prem/air-gap, compliance, audit, controlled deploy.” Strong transition: “What if discovery, risk, remediation, compliance were one continuous pipeline?”

▣ PLAN — DISCOVER + UNDERSTAND — say

“That is QIROVA.” Show simplified arch. STEP 1 DISCOVER: “Scan 5 sources: code, binaries, containers, network/TLS, deps. Visible or hidden in binary → one inventory.” STEP 2 UNDERSTAND: “Don't blindly trust pattern. Combine signals: structurally called API? library imported? key size? production? comment/test only? → confidence score.” Analogy: “‘Found word RSA’ vs ‘Strong evidence RSA-2048 in prod auth flow.’”

⬢ QUANTUM RISK — say

“We don't say ‘quantum vulnerable.’ We ask ‘How urgent is this asset?’ Combine attack cost, sensitivity, exposure, HNDL, migration timeline → Monte-Carlo P(exposure). Like ER triage: not every patient same. Killer line: “Instead of 10,000 findings: 20 now. 200 next. Rest low priority.””

✚ REMEDIATION — say

“Don't end with warning. Identify high-risk impl → propose PQC path e.g. ECC → ML-KEM, ML-DSA or hybrid. Don't blindly trust AI: generate → compile → test → rescan → verify. Fail confidence/validation = reject.”

✚ COMPLIANCE + OUTPUT — say

“Convert tech to usable: CBOM, risk reports, migration plans, compliance gaps, audit trail. Engineer gets findings. CISO gets priority. Auditor gets evidence. Management gets roadmap.”

★ CLOSING — slow, say

“Quantum threat isn't just breaking RSA/ECC. Real challenge: knowing where they are, which matter, when dangerous, how to replace safely. QIROVA: Discover. Understand. Quantify. Remediate. Verify. Prove compliance. Goal: Don't wait for quantum to discover you're not ready. Make enterprise quantum-ready before Q-Day. Thank you.”
